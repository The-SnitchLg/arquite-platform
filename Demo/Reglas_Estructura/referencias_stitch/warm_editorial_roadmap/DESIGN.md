## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes the artisanal, tactile spatial quality of modern creative editorial studios with the structured clarity, modular journey mapping, and pragmatic readability of contemporary strategic roadmap platforms. The brand voice balances architectural poise with human warmth: thoughtful, cultivated, optimistic, and luminous. 

Targeted toward creative strategists, architects, design directors, and vision-driven clients, the interface eschews cold enterprise tropes in favor of an expansive gallery feel. The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Warm Organic Tactility**:
- Soft alabaster and sand canvas tones provide an inviting foundation that reduces glare and fatigue.
- Structured modular grids offer linear legibility without rigid austerity.
- Dynamic typography creates tension between warm, literary display serifs and crisp geometric sans-serif structural elements.
- Interaction cues favor organic rounded containers, pill tags, and soft atmospheric depth over heavy drop shadows or abrupt strokes.

## Colors

The palette grounds the interface in calming, organic hues accented by botanical and mineral tones.

### Color Roles & Guidelines
- **Primary (`#0D9488`):** Deep sage teal, used for primary navigation accents, confirmed pathway nodes, authoritative links, and focused brand signifiers.
- **Secondary (`#EA580C`):** Sunlit terracotta, reserved for focal action points, milestones, callouts, alerts, and high-priority interactive cues.
- **Tertiary (`#2DD4BF`):** Ethereal mint, applied as a radiant touchpoint for active pills, progress tracking fills, and subtle focus halos.
- **Neutral (`#1E293B`):** Deep slate charcoal, replacing harsh solid blacks for all primary headlines, body text, and structural icons to maintain high contrast with organic softness.
- **Canvas & Surface Palette (`#FBFAF7`, `#F7F5F0`, `#FFFFFF`):** The primary background is warm sand-cream (`#FBFAF7`), layered with pure white (`#FFFFFF`) containers to establish elevation, while `#F7F5F0` defines recessed zones and side rails.
- **Soft Accent Tints (`#EEF3E7`, `#FCE7D9`):** Wash backgrounds for milestone chips, pathway progress tags, and curated callout blocks.
- **Borders (`#E9E6DF`):** Low-contrast architectural outlines preserving structure without visual clutter.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial atmosphere through intentional pairing:

1. **Newsreader (Display & Editorial Headlines):** Conveys literary sophistication, architectural narrative, and crafted poise. Used for primary page titles, major milestones, and pull quotes. Italics should be used sparingly for poetic emphasis.
2. **Plus Jakarta Sans (Functional Hierarchy & Body):** Delivers clean readability with gentle humanist curves. Ensures that roadmaps, modular timelines, complex forms, and informational metadata remain accessible and legible across all viewports.
3. **Labels & Micro-copy:** Set in uppercase or tight semi-bold tracking within pill shapes to indicate status, path numbering, and metadata filters.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an airy, 12-column responsive grid system designed to support horizontal roadmap flows and asymmetric editorial blocks.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with a maximum content container of `1320px`, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, and `3rem` (`48px`) outer margins. Editorial hero blocks allow wide 8/4 or 7/5 column splits.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2rem` outer margins. Multi-step horizontal pathways transition into scrolling horizontal carousels or stacked 2-column cards.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` outer margins. Roadmaps reflow to vertical step-lines connected with muted sage or sand tracking threads.
- **Rhythm & White Space:** Sections breathe using `space-2xl` (`4rem`) to `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) separations, creating an uncluttered gallery pace that encourages contemplation and clear strategic decision-making.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on warm tonal layering, architectural perimeter strokes, and soft atmospheric shadows rather than opaque, dark drop shadows.

- **Tonal Grounding:** Depth starts with the base canvas (`#FBFAF7`). Elevated cards and floating panels use pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a micro-border of `#E9E6DF`.
- **Atmospheric Warm Shadow (Cards & Modals):**
  - Card Resting: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04), 0 1px 3px -1px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.02)`
  - Card Hover & Milestone Active: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.06), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.03)`
  - Dialog / Overlay: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`
- **Backdrop Filters:** Glass overlays (such as sticky headers and floating roadmap toolbars) employ `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over a 85% opacity `#FBFAF7` tint to subtly blur underlying content while maintaining a warm paper feel.

## Shapes

The shape system blends structured discipline with gentle organic curvatures:
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Cards):** Built on a foundation of `roundedness: 2` (`0.5rem` / `8px` for inputs and standard buttons; `1rem` / `16px` for primary pathway cards).
- **Major Modular Blocks & Surfaces:** Roadmap containers and editorial highlight cards extend to `1.5rem` (`24px`) or `2rem` (`32px`) radii, providing a friendly, tactile presence.
- **Capsules & Pills:** Status tokens, metadata badges, pathway step indicators, and primary pill actions use full radius (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) to maintain contrast against rectangular content blocks.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in deep teal (`#0D9488`) with text in `#FFFFFF`. Soft pill or `rounded-lg` geometry. Hover shifts smoothly to an enriched shade with a subtle terracotta or mint under-glow.
- **Secondary / Action:** Terracotta fill (`#EA580C`) or terracotta stroke over white for high-priority interactive branches and launch triggers.
- **Subtle / Ghost:** Pure white background with `#E9E6DF` border, slate text (`#1E293B`), transitioning to `#F7F5F0` on hover.

### Chips & Pathway Badges
- Fully rounded pills (`rounded-full`) with `label-sm` or `label-md` typography.
- Statuses use chromatic tints:
  - Active / In Progress: `#EEF3E7` surface, `#0D9488` text, mint outline accent.
  - Milestone / Urgent: `#FCE7D9` surface, `#EA580C` text.
  - Informational / Topic: `#F7F5F0` surface, `#64748B` text.

### Modular Pathway Cards
- Constructed with `#FFFFFF` background, a 1px border of `#E9E6DF`, and generous internal padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).
- Features a structured header block: category pill + phase numbering, editorial headline in Newsreader or Plus Jakarta Sans, concise narrative summary, and an integrated bottom progress bar or checkpoint avatar stack.

### Inputs & Form Elements
- Inputs feature `#FFFFFF` fill with an `#E9E6DF` resting border, `0.75rem` vertical padding, and `rounded-lg` (`8px`) corners.
- Focus state replaces the border with `#0D9488` accompanied by a soft 3px ambient ring of `rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.25)`.
- Checkboxes and radio controls feature pill and smooth circular designs; checked states fill with deep teal and clear white checkmarks.

### Roadmaps & Timeline Nodes
- Continuous hairline connector tracks in `#E9E6DF` with active sequences filled in `#0D9488` or animated mint gradients.
- Interactive nodes feature white circular medallions with colored central pips and numerical step counters, expanding on hover to reveal editorial modal previews.
